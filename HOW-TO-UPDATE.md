# Adding a project or a review

Go to **yoursite.com/admin** and log in. Works on your phone.

You'll see one page: **Projects, reviews & Instagram**.

## Add a project

Scroll to **Projects**, tap **Add project**, fill in:

- **Job name** — short. "Timber framed patio cover", not "Beautiful custom outdoor living space"
- **Type of work** — pick one. This is what the filter buttons on the Projects page use
- **Town**, **Year**
- **Photo** — tap, upload straight from your camera roll. Landscape shots work best
- **What the job was** — a sentence or two. What you actually did. Specifics sell better
  than adjectives: "new slab with perimeter footings, ran new power and water" beats
  "quality craftsmanship"

Tap **Publish** at the top. The site updates in about a minute.

The newest three projects also show on the home page, so if you want a job featured, drag
it to the top of the list.

## Add a review

Same page, scroll to **Reviews**. Paste what they said, then who said it — first name,
last initial, town. If it came in as a text message, paste it word for word. Don't clean
it up.

## Change a photo

Projects → tap the job → tap the photo → upload a new one → Publish.

## Reorder anything

Drag the ⠿ handle on the left of any item.

## Delete something

Tap the item, then the trash can.

---

**One rule:** hit **Publish**, not just back. Nothing goes live until you publish.

If something looks broken after you publish, nothing is lost — the previous version is
still saved and can be restored.
